1️⃣  Upload testing scripts to VPS:

   $ ls -l test_redis*.sh setup_redis*.sh
   
2️⃣  Make scripts executable:
   $ chmod +x test_redis_connection.sh
   $ chmod +x test_redis_simple.sh
   $ chmod +x setup_redis_config.sh

3️⃣  Auto-configure .env:
   $ ./setup_redis_config.sh

   - Update .env automatically

7️⃣  Verify Redis connection in logs:
